# The Quiet Dread 0.10 — Trail legacy

Three-unit Trail starts, seeded encounter routes and opponents, recruitment on the march, army growth up to twenty formations, survivor training and a final commander-and-titan boss hunt are implemented.

Faction-specific completion unlocks persist separately from the campaign and apply to Trail and Conquest. Verminhorde rewards: Gore-Maidens after one win, Seraphyx after two, God-Queen after ten. Other factions earn an elite, alternate commanders and a titan on those milestones. Skirmish stays unrestricted. The catalogue now contains 243 profiles.

See TRAIL-PROGRESSION.md for controls, economy, save compatibility, source attribution and limitations. This release retains the optional local Ollama narrator from 0.9; unlock outcomes are included in its authoritative campaign ledger.

Validation: the existing rules, terrain, coherence, battle-polish and narration suites plus dedicated progression tests. Full-run progression tests simulate tactical victories; they establish state correctness, not human-play balance. Runtime smoke evidence is in QA/runtime-smoke.txt. Long-term economy and boss difficulty remain playtesting tasks.
