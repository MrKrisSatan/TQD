# Titans of The Quiet Dread

All titan mechanics and tactical statistics are original adaptations. One titan per army, within the 1,600-point limit. Large visual scale does not change the one-cell logical footprint. Their size imposes +10 enemy accuracy. Signature powers cost 2 AP and are usable once per turn, except the siege engine’s brace action, which costs 1 AP and permits a subsequent shot. Spawned troops do not become permanent campaign recruits.

## Verminhorde Dominion — Verminia, the Walking Womb-Palace

Birth a Clawrat formation every turn at no Aether cost, provided an adjacent cell and active brood slot are free. Destroyed broods free slots.

Counterplay: Block adjacent birth cells or kill the All-Mother. At most six spawned broods can be alive; no lifetime birth limit.
