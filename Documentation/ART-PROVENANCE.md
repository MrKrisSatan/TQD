## Runtime visuals

`Assets/Scripts/BattleVisuals.cs` constructs terrain, ruins, relay towers, formation miniatures, tanks, artillery, faction details and titan silhouettes from Unity primitives. These are original code-generated models. No SOVL, Warhammer or OPR model, texture, logo or sound was extracted or copied.

The low-poly schematic presentation is intentional for this prototype; it does not claim to be finished sculpted character art. Walkers, beasts, daemon forms, a rat queen, a prism construct, a tree elder, a knight, tomb and cathedral forms express the different titan roles. Faction colours and additional geometry differentiate related chassis.

Impact sounds are original decaying sine-wave clips synthesized at runtime. There is no music or voice acting.

## Faction illustrations

Seventeen separate imagegen requests produced the representative faction leader paintings in Assets/Resources/FactionArt. Exact individual prompts and original output locations are recorded in FACTION-ART-PROMPTS.json. These paintings are original visual adaptations, not codex source illustrations or claims about the canonical appearance of every named general. Commander variants share their faction painting. The supplied SOVL screenshots informed composition and layout only.

The interface uses the installed Windows Georgia/Times New Roman font at runtime, falling back through the OS font list. No Windows font files are redistributed. Unit thumbnails are rendered in Unity from the original procedural models; ground grain is generated by the game's terrain material code.

## Battlefield ground and foliage — 0.6.0

Original imagegen assets, generated 18 October 2026, copied into Assets/Resources/BattleGround.png and TreeCanopy.png. Originals remain in the imagegen output directory. Textures are used on native Unity terrain and alpha-cutout tree canopies; geometry, cover and collision rules are implemented separately.

Ground prompt:
> A seamless tileable top-down strategy-game ground texture, square image. Orthographic straight-down view of realistic dark olive grass, dry moss, patches of compact brown earth, tiny pale gravel, subtle worn paths and leaf litter. Fine richly painted natural detail like a high quality tabletop battlefield viewed overhead. Restrained muted military olive and umber colours, fairly dark, even ambient lighting with no cast shadows. Absolutely no trees, no rocks larger than small pebbles, no buildings, no objects, no text, no borders, no grid, no perspective. Designed to tile across terrain in a science fantasy strategy game.

Tree prompt:
> One isolated top-down tree canopy sprite for an overhead strategy game. Orthographic view looking vertically down at a mature broadleaf woodland tree. Dense richly detailed small olive-green leaves, natural irregular lobed circular silhouette, subtle twig structure, deep shadow between foliage clumps and softly lit yellow-green tips, realistic hand-painted game art. Canopy fills 85 percent of a square canvas with generous transparent margin on every side. No visible trunk from this overhead viewpoint, no ground, no cast shadow outside the canopy, no text, no frame. Transparent background outside the irregular foliage silhouette.

Ground texture and tree canopy are original assets; the user's SOVL battle screenshots were visual references only. Rocks are original faceted meshes; buildings, ruins and walls use native geometry.

## 0.7 modular miniatures
Original runtime geometry authored in Assets/Scripts/Miniatures.cs. Shared primitive parts are arranged into faction anatomy, armour, weapons, mounts, engines and titan details, then combined by material. No external model downloads or extracted game models. These are modular procedural models, not individually hand-sculpted assets.
