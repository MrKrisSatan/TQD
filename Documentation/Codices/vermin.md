# Verminhorde Dominion

Beneath every throne, a thousand guns.

Break the line with massed rifles and siege shells. Send Blackclaws through the tunnels, harvest enemy memories, and feed the next brood.

Adapted from the supplied Verminhorde codex.

## The established Dominion

The Verminhorde Dominion replaces the Ratkin Clans completely. Its authoritative faction reference remains the user-provided Codex Verminhorde Dominion Definitive 11th Edition v1.1. This expansion does not rename Verminia, replace Varkrul or manufacture a contradictory founding myth. The original source describes a subterranean empire of mass infantry, artillery, scavenged armour, tunnel warfare and intelligence theft, held together by brood production and competing authorities.

Verminia the All-Mother stands above the Thirteen Daughters, Great Clans, military commanders and technical institutions. Varkrul’s warfare targets the systems that keep an enemy functional rather than merely its strongest warrior. The Dread-Confessors and Feast of Memory turn death into intelligence, while the Scrap-Seers recombine captured technologies. These source-derived features remain the standard against which the new factions have been differentiated.

## Strength and its cost

The source makes the Dominion powerful but constrained. Accelerated population replacement consumes biomass; hybrid technology is hard to maintain; tunnels can be flooded or collapsed; clan competition wastes resources; memory rites carry contamination risks. These costs matter because an army with infinite replacements, perfect stolen technology and unanswerable tunnels would offer little strategic interaction.

The current game represents only part of this depth: Pressure, Broken enemies, artillery, sabotage, memory harvest, engineering repair, tunnel travel and a limited Chitterhold replacement. The tactical spell powers assigned to Confessors are explicitly original Ashcrown adaptations, not claims about the source datasheet. The detailed page mapping and omissions remain in CODEX-MAPPING.md.

## The Dominion in the Ashcrown setting

Relations with the twelve other powers are new setting material. The Dominion competes with the Kharad for deep infrastructure, with the Cinderjaw for salvage and with the Continuum and Vesperglass over the value and ownership of memory. It threatens the Compact by entering beneath civic systems and the Synod by altering water and soil unseen. Sauridian archives and Lumen predictive instruments are especially attractive technical prizes.

Neither holy nor corrupted sisterhood is merely another source of expendable infantry. The Dawn’s credibility can interrupt the dependency networks on which hidden warrens rely; the Hollow Star’s soul claims conflict with the Dominion’s practical treatment of all life as resource. These tensions create new campaign premises without retroactively placing the original codex’s external-setting wars inside Ashcrown’s chronology.

Source p.9: a forge world devoted to dismantling and rebuilding captured technology.

Source p.9: a Rotlung laboratory world of chemical trials and hidden infrastructure.

Source p.35: artillery foundries and siege schools. In-game doctrine increases suppression from artillery hits.

## Deepdelve

Source p.39: tunnel infrastructure and reserve assaults. In-game doctrine expands tunnel eligibility and reduces travel AP cost.

## Chitterhold

Source p.42: replacement warfare. In-game doctrine returns one lost Clawrat formation at half strength for Biomass.

## The remaining Great Clans

All thirteen remain part of the supplied lore. Ten clan packages and most of their tactical orders are not yet mechanically implemented; their existence is not replaced by the three available doctrines.

## Verminia the All-Mother

Source pp.14–15 and 89: the sovereign and biological centre of the Dominion. The Quiet Dread adds a playable colossal manifestation and renewable brood mechanic as an original adaptation.

## Varkrul Thirteenclaw

Source pp.26–28 and 89. The established warlord whose Breaking Number doctrine attacks enemy systems. His full character profile is not implemented in the current battle roster.

## Seraphyx Thirteen-Veil

Source p.111 supersedes p.90. The Watching Daughter is playable using an adapted Watcher’s Dossier and venom needler. Her game-role commander eligibility and sabotage are original adaptations.

## Source chronology

The codex’s Ashen Reach, Red Birthing, Mirehollow and Lathros campaigns remain in its original chronology. They are not silently relabelled as Ash Calendar events.

## New premise — The Stolen Meridian

A Dominion retrieval force discovers that a Sauridian reactor is part of a wider living maintenance network. The operation becomes a choice between taking a component and awakening the system around it.

## New premise — The Memory Siege

Vesperglass, Continuum and Dominion forces contest a damaged archive containing incompatible records of the same battle. Each side wants a different kind of victory from the same evidence.

## Playable roster

### War-Marshal

Commander · 65 points · HP 20 · Armour 2

A field commander who turns expendable broods into a coordinated battle line.

Battle Orders: an ally within 4 gains +1 control and +15 accuracy this turn.

### Clawrat Brood

Line infantry · 65 points · HP 24 · Armour 0

Numerous, lightly armoured trench infantry. Their losses still buy information.

Expendable Sensors: dying on an objective adds Pressure to a nearby enemy.

### Stormvermin Cohort

Veteran infantry · 135 points · HP 28 · Armour 3

Veteran riflemen and power-halberd troops who hold the breach.

Hold the Line: +1 armour while on an objective.

### Blackclaw Cell

Infiltrator · 120 points · HP 20 · Armour 2

Elite clandestine operators with stolen accelerators and demolition charges.

Sabotage: an enemy within 4 and near an objective gains Pressure. Can use tunnels.

### Dread-Confessor

Memory / magic · 75 points · HP 18 · Armour 1

Fear officers and keepers of the Feast of Memory. This adaptation makes their sermons supernatural.

Harvest 1 Memory per round from nearby infantry deaths (max 3). Dread Pulse is an original magic adaptation.

### Scrap-Seer

Engineer · 70 points · HP 18 · Armour 1

An engineer-scientist who regards enemy equipment as tomorrow’s spare parts.

Field Repair: restore 7 HP to a nearby vehicle. Spend Salvage automatically for 4 extra HP.

### Sapper Claw

Combat engineer · 95 points · HP 24 · Armour 1

Tunnel builders and trench engineers, skilled enough to be worth bringing home.

Fortify: create a trench on your tile. Can use tunnel exits.

### Gas-Claw Cohort

Chemical infantry · 105 points · HP 24 · Armour 1

Sealed assault troops who turn close terrain into choking kill zones.

Ignores cover; wounds slow infantry for their next turn.

### Gnasher Siege Mortar

Indirect artillery · 105 points · HP 24 · Armour 2

Enormous-calibre shells arc over the walls that stop ordinary rifles.

Deploy before firing. Indirect blast hits adjacent formations, including allies. Applies Pressure.

### Plagueburst Mortar

Chemical artillery · 125 points · HP 26 · Armour 2

Rotlung artillery spreads chemical denial over the battlefield.

Indirect blast ignores cover and slows infantry. Deploy before firing.

### Warrantank

Battle tank · 160 points · HP 38 · Armour 5

A broad-tracked accumulation of stolen guns and welded armour.

Patchwork Armour: first incoming attack each round loses 2 damage.

### Rat Ogre Pack

Assault brutes · 110 points · HP 34 · Armour 1

Augmented breachers that turn biology into close-range ordnance.

Charge: move up to movement +1 to strike; adds 4 damage. Costs 2 AP.

### Seraphyx Thirteen-Veil

Named commander · 105 points · HP 22 · Armour 2

The Watching Daughter, an intelligence master acting with the Mother’s authority.

Watcher’s Dossier: one enemy starts with Pressure. Uses the v1.1 annex profile as reference.

### Verminia, the Walking Womb-Palace

Colossal All-Mother · 500 points · HP 115 · Armour 5

Verminia the All-Mother is codex-derived (pp.14–15,89). Her walking battlefield manifestation, titan statistics and renewable brood rules are original game adaptations requested by the user.

Birth a Clawrat formation every turn at no Aether cost, provided an adjacent cell and active brood slot are free. Destroyed broods free slots.


## Gore-Maidens — native campaign reward

Original game cadre inspired by the Slaughter-Maidens of codex pp.16–17. Stealth, mono-blades, venom needlers and sabotage; 140 points. The first Verminhorde Trail victory unlocks recruitment in both campaigns. These statistics and the unlock rule are adaptations, not codex canon.
