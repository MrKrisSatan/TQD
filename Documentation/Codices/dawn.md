# Daughters of the Unbroken Dawn

No life is beneath the light.

An all-female holy military order. Armoured sisters, healing saints and radiant engines defend the helpless without demanding conversion.

Original Ashcrown faction.

## The covenant of the unbroken light

The Daughters of the Unbroken Dawn are an all-female holy military order founded after a catastrophe known as the Long Eclipse. When a relay failure darkened several inhabited worlds, hospitals and evacuation stations became the first targets of desperate armies. A network of women physicians, engineers, guards and spiritual teachers formed mutual-defence houses around those refuges. Their first sacred promise was practical: no wounded person would be surrendered to an aggressor because helping them was politically inconvenient.

The earliest miracles occurred during the defence of a refuge whose generators had failed. Witnesses described a steady light that protected the shelter without burning those outside it. The Daughters call its source the Unbroken Light, a benevolent transcendence that answers freely undertaken acts of protection. They do not claim to own it. Their theology holds that a miracle obtained through coercion is already something else, regardless of how radiant it appears.

## Women sworn to shelter

Every sworn member of the faction is a woman: commanders, rifle sisters, scouts, pilots, artillery crews, engineers, healers and living saints. Recruitment is voluntary and open across human cultures and social backgrounds. The order’s women-only covenant is its institutional tradition, not a claim that other people are lesser or incapable of holiness. Allied men and nonmembers may receive protection, employment or political representation in the communities it serves, but are not disguised male combat units within its roster.

The Houses of Shelter elect a High Matriarch for a fixed term. Independent sisters called Witnesses audit conduct, finances and the treatment of prisoners. A sister may challenge an unlawful command without forfeiting her vows. Leaving the order is permitted after an orderly transfer of duties. Their discipline is demanding because goodness requires responsibility, but it does not depend on humiliating penitents, forced conversion or manufacturing martyrs.

## Faith expressed through action

Dawn devotion is measured by truthful conduct, care for the vulnerable and restraint toward a defeated enemy. Their holy character is genuine within the setting. They are not secretly another predatory empire waiting for a darker reveal. This does not make them omniscient. Sisters can misjudge evidence, exhaust supplies or disagree bitterly over which threatened community they can reach first. Their moral struggle concerns limited power and conflicting duties, rather than a hidden conviction that cruelty is virtue.

Miracles require coherent Aether, trained intention and a freely accepted burden. They cannot be mass-produced by ordering civilians to pray. Hospitals therefore keep ordinary medicine, factories maintain spare parts and quartermasters plan for miracles to fail. Saints are examined by Witnesses rather than granted absolute authority. The order honours those who die protecting others while teaching that surviving to protect again is not a lesser form of courage.

## Armour, flame and mercy

The army combines disciplined armoured sisters with radiant firearms, shield units, mobile lancers and carefully controlled artillery. Purifying Flame Sisters destroy demonic anchors and dangerous war materiel; their doctrine forbids treating an inhabited district as a convenient cleansing target. Sanctuary Engines carry women crews and practical rescue equipment. Saints and Sisters of the Living Light support the same soldiers who maintain the order’s mundane defence network.

Merciful Radiance makes protective Ward magic cheaper, improves nearby recovery from suppression and grants Benediction healing. This creates a resilient combined-arms force, but it remains expensive and can be outnumbered. Aether shortages, anti-armour fire and divided rescue obligations create real weaknesses. In the current battle game the artillery still uses the common blast rules, including friendly fire; holiness does not exempt the player from responsible targeting.

## The wound called the Hollow Star

The Hollow Star began with a faction of sisters who decided that protection should confer ownership and that withheld miracles justified stealing power. The Daughters remember the betrayal without treating every survivor as permanently beyond rescue. They offer surrender to mortals who can still choose freely, while opposing the Convent’s leaders and predatory manifestations without romanticising them.

The Daughters cooperate with the Compact, Hornbound, Synod and others when civilian lives can be protected. They will also oppose those allies if the alliance demands atrocity. The Continuum’s consensual restoration is debated respectfully rather than automatically condemned as evil; the relevant question is whether the person remains free. Their strongest strategic asset is trust built through costly consistency. Their enemies exploit that trust by creating multiple emergencies, knowing the sisters cannot simply stop caring when care becomes inconvenient.

## Aster’s Refuge

The original hospital-world now holds the order’s principal Witness council. Its central sanctuary has doors facing every landing field and no private entrance for dignitaries.

## Dawnward Bastion

A fortress and evacuation port built to shelter populations rather than merely garrison troops. Its outer defences can be sacrificed without cutting off the hospital districts.

## The Lantern Pilgrimage

A network of mobile relief fleets and small houses on endangered worlds. Many sisters spend their entire service here without visiting the ceremonial capital.

## Order of the Open Hand

Healers and protective infantry prioritise evacuation, medicine and prisoner safety. They are sometimes accused of surrendering tactical advantage to obligations other armies would ignore.

## Order of the Vigilant Sword

Armoured battle sisters specialise in disciplined counterattack against threats to protected communities. Their internal debates concern when pre-emptive force is truly necessary.

## Order of the Far Lantern

Scouts, pilots and mounted sisters defend remote routes. Their isolation demands independent judgement and makes reliable evidence more precious than impressive doctrine.

## Order of the Unbroken Seal

Artificers, wardsmiths and exo-armour crews contain demonic breaches. They train specifically to distinguish corruption from unfamiliar but harmless spiritual traditions.

## The Witness Houses

Independent investigators serve throughout the order. Their authority to question saints and matriarchs is treated as a sacred protection against the return of Vaelora’s error.

## High Matriarch Elyra Solenne

Elyra was a surgeon during a war in which both sides claimed her hospital’s patients as enemies. She armed its evacuation teams and refused to release either group to execution. Elected Matriarch for her judgement rather than miraculous power, she regards every battle plan as a list of lives entrusted to her. Her public disagreements with Serene are preserved in the order’s archives so that future sisters can learn how principled people resolve competing duties.

## Marshal Serene Valecourt

Serene commands the order’s largest field host. She once abandoned a near-certain pursuit victory to recover prisoners from a collapsing enemy transport, allowing the defeated commander to escape. Critics still invoke the decision; the rescued people do too. She is neither naive nor gentle toward an active threat. Her skill lies in using overwhelming local force without allowing the convenience of violence to decide its proper limits.

## Saint-Errant Iona Vey

Iona’s first recorded miracle restored light to an evacuation corridor while she remained behind to guide the last wounded through it. She refuses permanent command because she fears people will obey her radiance when they should examine her reasoning. Her power is strongest in direct protection and healing. She travels with ordinary supply crews and insists that the names of the women who repair her transport appear beside hers in mission records.

## 62 AC — The Long Eclipse

Refuge houses formed a mutual-defence covenant during a cascade of relay failures. The first shelter miracle became the order’s spiritual foundation.

## 198 AC — The Broken Benediction

Vaelora’s faction attempted to compel miracles through an oath-engine. The resulting schism created the Hollow Star and forced the Daughters to formalise independent Witness oversight.

## 307 AC — The Shelter of Unpaid Names

Sisters defended escaped memory debtors against Vesperglass claims. The campaign established a protected testimony archive that charges no price for a person’s own history.

## 312 AC — The Dawnward Vigil

Elyra divides her forces between sealing a demonic breach and rescuing civilians deliberately trapped to draw her away. The operation is a current campaign premise, not a predestined victory.
